The logos retain their downloaded artwork and brand colours. CSS contains their dimensions, with the full mark visible in every logo slot. The strip scrolls horizontally in a continuous loop and supports native swiping and keyboard scrolling. Automatic movement pauses on hover, keyboard focus, interaction and when the strip is outside the viewport. Reduced motion preferences disable automatic scrolling. Each logo has the brand name as its alternative text; the duplicate loop is hidden from assistive technology.

These marks identify the corresponding brands. Their inclusion does not establish brand affiliation or authorisation; product supply remains subject to individual dealers.
